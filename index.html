Dishcovery

I love to travel and try new food, but menus in other languages always left me guessing. I'd end up looking up every dish one by one just to figure out what I was even ordering. So I made Dishcovery: point your phone at a menu and it tells you what each dish actually is, not just a literal translation. It gives you the cultural context behind it too, like the taste, texture, spice level, and where it's from. It also has a city guide for must-try local dishes and eating customs.

Python, FastAPI, Pydantic, OpenAI vision/LLM, vanilla JS/HTML/CSS
